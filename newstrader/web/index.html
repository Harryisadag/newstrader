Welcome to NewsTrader

Three quick steps and it starts watching the news (in PAPER mode - no real money):

  1. Add your keys - Alpaca paper key + secret. (An Anthropic key is only needed if you switch the AI engine to Claude - the default local engine is free.)
  2. Check everything works - Logs → Run diagnostics. Each red line says how to fix it.
  3. Let it run. Headlines and TV transcripts show up here, AI calls in Signals, paper trades in Portfolio, and whether the AI is right in Performance.

Local ML model

The free local engine has two layers. FinBERT reads the wording of each headline (good / bad / neutral). The price model learns from your Alpaca history how stocks actually moved compared with the S&P 500 in the hour after similar headlines. Training is free, downloads a few minutes of data per month of history, and is tested on the newest 20% of headlines it never saw while learning.

New backtest

Replays historical Alpaca/Benzinga news through the same pre-filter, AI engine and validator, then simulates your current stop-loss / take-profit and risk settings on minute-by-minute prices. Free with the local engine; costs Claude credits with Claude.
